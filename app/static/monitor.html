Web Monitor Automation

Watches a competitor's online store, detects price and stock changes, and sends alerts. The "competitor" is Gadget Hub, a fictional store served by this demo, with 30 simulated days of real-looking changes.

How it works, and what to try
  1. Scrape: fetches the store page and reads each product with CSS selectors (name, price, stock).
  2. Compare: diffs against the previous snapshot: price drops and rises, stock-outs, restocks, new and removed products.
  3. Alert: applies your threshold rule, then formats Slack, email and webhook notifications (dry run here).
