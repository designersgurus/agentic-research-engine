Document AI Extraction

Turns invoices, receipts and purchase orders into structured data, then checks the maths before anything is approved. Clean documents are auto-approved; problems go to a human with the exact reasons.

How it works, and what to try
  1. Read: typed text, a PDF, or a photo of the document (JPG, PNG, WebP). Photos and scans go through OCR.
  2. Extract: document type, vendor, number, dates, bill-to, currency, line items, subtotal, tax, total, each with a confidence score.
  3. Validate: required fields, qty × price per line, lines add up to subtotal, subtotal + tax = total, tax matches the stated rate, due date after issue date.
  4. Decide: auto-approve, or send to review with reasons. Export as JSON or CSV.
Try Invoice (with errors): it contains three problems. Or upload a photo or PDF of your own invoice (up to 5 MB). Files are processed in memory and never stored.
