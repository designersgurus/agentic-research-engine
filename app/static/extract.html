Document AI Extraction

Turns invoices, receipts and purchase orders into structured data, then checks the maths before anything is approved. Clean documents are auto-approved; problems go to a human with the exact reasons.

How it works, and what to try
  1. Extract: document type, vendor, number, dates, bill-to, currency, line items, subtotal, tax, total, each with a confidence score.
  2. Validate: required fields, qty × price per line, lines add up to subtotal, subtotal + tax = total, tax matches the stated rate, due date after issue date.
  3. Decide: auto-approve, or send to review with reasons. Export as JSON or CSV.
Try Invoice (with errors): it contains three problems. Or upload your own text-based PDF invoice (up to 2 MB).
